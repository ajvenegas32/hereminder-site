HereMinder reminds you of things where you can actually do them — milk at any grocery store, a hammer at any hardware store, the trash cans when you pull up at home. Reminders aren't firing when I arrive at a store

HereMinder needs your location permission set to Always so it can remind you while the app is in the background, which is when arrivals usually happen. Open the iOS Settings app > HereMinder > Location, and make sure it's set to Always and that Precise Location is on. Arrival reminders also rely on iOS geofencing, which can take a short while to register a new place and works best with a good GPS signal.

How does HereMinder know what store sells my item?

When you add an item, HereMinder tags it with the kind of store it's usually sold at — milk at the grocery store, a hammer at the hardware store. Big-box stores carry most of it, so one stop can clear your whole list in a single trip. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, an on-device model helps tell ambiguous stores apart; on other iPhones it uses Apple Maps place types and a built-in list of well-known brands.

An item was tagged for the wrong kind of store

Tap Not right? under a just-added item, pick the store type, then choose Always remember so it sticks for every future add. You can also add a rule any time in Settings > Custom stores & rules. Editing an item changes just that one item.

What's included free, and what's in Pro?

HereMinder is free to use, and every new install gets full Pro for 14 days so you can feel it work in your routine. After that, the free tier keeps the everyday essentials: automatic store-type tagging, custom stores and rules, up to 10 active location reminders, and unlimited pinned places. HereMinder Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription, ever — that unlocks unlimited active reminders, iCloud sync, multiple and shared lists, and list sorting.

Some of my reminders say they won't fire

The free tier keeps 10 location reminders armed at once. Check off or delete an item to free a slot, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited reminders. Nothing is lost — after you free a slot, swipe the paused item and tap Reactivate.

Sharing a list

With Pro, you can share a list and invite family through iCloud. Everyone you invite joins free, can add and check off items, and gets reminded at the right stores. Stop sharing at any time from the same menu; only lists you explicitly share are ever shared.

I bought Pro but don't see it on my other device

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then open HereMinder, tap the Pro row at the top of your list (HereMinder Pro — or Pro trial during the first 14 days), and tap Restore purchase. iCloud sync also needs to be turned on for your list to appear across devices.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your list stays on your iPhone, there are no ads or trackers, and nothing about your list or your stores is sent to us. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where does the store data come from?

HereMinder ships with a table of stores built from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, offered under the Open Database License. The table and the program that builds it are on the store data page.
